# Text And CLI Preparation

Use a new Codex desktop task or an interactive `codex` terminal session. Both load the same agent-led skills. No separate terminal application or shared browser database is included.

## Interview And Planning

Invoke `tpir-prep-assistant`. Provide a timeline, known theme, group size, and personal details you want to rehearse. Missing information should not prevent a general plan. Concrete taping logistics require a fresh official-source check.

The agent asks one short interview question, waits for your reply, suggests one concrete improvement, and lets you rehearse again. Continue with occupation, hometown, favorite game, dream prize, and group questions. Aim for authentic 15-30 second answers. Finish with a short script, schedule, and pricing homework. Coaching is not a prediction of casting success.

```text
Use $tpir-prep-assistant. My coworkers and I are going to a holiday taping. Rehearse my interview one question at a time.
```

## Game Practice

Invoke `tpir-game-practice` for a category, named game, or full pipeline. The agent loads relevant references, verifies unfamiliar rules, generates a fresh scenario, and fixes the hidden answer before accepting bids. It shows product features and information available at the current decision.

```text
Use $tpir-game-practice. Run Cliff Hangers with one product and one bid at a time.
```

Choose one price, digit, bid, or other legal action at a time. Forced events play out automatically. Full games allow up to six Contestants Row attempts under the initial-four convention, then a pricing game, Big Wheel, and Showcase if you advance. A pricing-game loss does not eliminate you from the wheel. Outcomes appear before the next decision; request explicit review pauses if desired.

The Big Wheel is the Showcase Showdown; final bundle bidding is the Showcase. The agent uses context to disambiguate.

Standalone drills do not aggregate into full-game scores. Full-game attempts, gates, prizes, score, and dollar totals stay in the conversation. Resume in the same thread, or bring a recap to a new one. Conversational state does not sync with browser history and is not a deterministic replay system.
