## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-23

Initial public release of the TPIR practice project.

- Provides both a Codex-compatible preparation skill and a static mobile web app.
- Runs the full practice pipeline through Contestants Row, a pricing game, the Big Wheel, and the Showcase.
- Includes standalone, untracked practice launchers and browser-session history and reporting.
- Adds incremental simulators for Any Number, Cliff Hangers, Plinko, Five Price Tags, and Vend-O-Price.
- Keeps hidden prices private until the applicable decision resolves and validates known answer-leak patterns.
- Includes responsive mobile navigation, stage visuals, product-category images, and LAN serving instructions.
