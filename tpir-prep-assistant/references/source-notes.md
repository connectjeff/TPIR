# Source Notes

Use these notes to ground TPIR preparation advice. Cite the original sources when making claims that are not common knowledge. Re-check live sources for dates, locations, tickets, eligibility, special tapings, and rules because they change.

## Official And Current-Detail Sources

- Official TPIR site: https://priceisright.com/  
  Useful for current show links, official game pages, cast, giveaway/ticket links, and contact/accessibility information.

- Official TPIR games list: https://priceisright.com/games/  
  Use this as the primary source for which pricing games are recognized by the show. Individual pages, such as Golden Road at https://priceisright.com/game/golden-road/, give concise gameplay summaries.

- On Camera Audiences TPIR ticket page: https://on-camera-audiences.com/shows/the-price-is-right/  
  Use for current ticket availability, age requirement, special taping notes, and audience logistics. At time of research, the page said tickets are generally released about a month in advance, show dates may be subject to change, attendees must be 18+, and special taping themes can be announced.

## Contestant Selection Sources

- TV Insider, "How 'Price Is Right' Contestants Are Picked for the Game Show" (June 29, 2024): https://www.tvinsider.com/1141937/the-price-is-right-how-contestants-are-picked/  
  Includes executive producer Evelyn Warfel's explanation that production assistants conduct very brief pre-show interviews to assess energy and personality, often asking about favorite games, hometown, and what the show means to the attendee. Use as a high-value current source.

- CBS Philadelphia contestant-search article (July 2026): https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/the-price-is-right-contestant-search-philadelphia-july-22-2026/  
  Useful because it publishes casting tips from producers for a regional audition: name, age, hometown, occupation, favorite game, dream prize, why the person would be a good contestant, and a "Come on down" delivery. It also emphasizes enthusiasm, authenticity, volume, fun, not being aggressive, and some pricing knowledge.

- Former contestant Aurora De Lucia's blog post, "Q: How Do I Become a Contestant on The Price is Right?" (April 22, 2013): https://aurorasblog.com/2013/04/22/q-how-do-i-become-a-contestant-on-the-price-is-right/  
  Anecdotal, useful for interview framing. She recommends a memorable shirt, going with others, showing personality, and treating "what do you do?" or "where are you from?" as an invitation to explain what makes you distinct in under a minute.

- Former contestant/taping report, "Stose on TPIR: What happens at a Price is Right taping" (April 2019): https://stoseontpir.blogspot.com/2019/04/what-happens-at-price-is-right-taping.html  
  Anecdotal, useful for process texture: paperwork, ID checks, photos, waiting, interview groups, brief interaction time, and having a real answer ready for favorite game.

- Oakland County Moms interview with former contestant Eliot (November 2024): https://www.oaklandcountymoms.com/behind-the-scenes-of-a-price-is-right-taping-51934/  
  Anecdotal. Reinforces that the screening interaction can be very short and that clear, upbeat self-presentation matters.

- TV Insider contestant advice article (May 2025): https://www.tvinsider.com/1188962/the-price-is-right-contestant-reveals-how-get-chosen-show-secrets/  
  Anecdotal from contestant Krupali. Use for practical advice such as being fun, enthusiastic, energetic, dancing/meeting people, and being there to enjoy the experience.

## Strategy And Research Sources

- Berk, Hughson, and Vandezande, "The Price Is Right, But Are the Bids? An Empirical Investigation of Rational Decision Making," American Economic Review, 1996: https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/price-right-are-bids-empirical-investigation-rational-decision-making  
  Use when explaining Contestants Row and Showcase bidding behavior. The study found suboptimal bidding patterns and discusses simple rules of thumb and bounded rationality. Do not overstate it as a perfect modern strategy guide because formats, prizes, and eras can differ.

- Fan-maintained TPIR Wiki season timelines, such as Season 52: https://priceisright.fandom.com/wiki/The_Price_is_Right_Timeline/Season_52  
  Use only as fan-maintained episode-history context, especially for holiday-week ambience and special episodes. Treat as non-official.

## Stable Preparation Takeaways

- Selection: Prepare a concise, authentic personal hook; know your favorite game; be energetic without being pushy; listen during the interview process; make the shirt/group/theme memorable but not distracting.
- Pricing: Practice retail prices by category and compare relative prices. Study current store prices for grocery items, household goods, electronics, appliances, furniture, trips, and vehicles.
- Contestants Row: The core rule is closest bid without going over. Account for prior bids and the risk of overbidding; exact "one dollar over" tactics are situational and depend on bid order and confidence.
- Showcase: Build bundle-estimation skill, not single-item recall. Practice summing trips, cars, furniture, appliances, and bonus items quickly.
- Themed tapings: Themes can affect audience wardrobe, on-set energy, and sometimes prize framing. They do not let the user know which games or prizes will appear.
